New · For Mac

Knob

Volume control, the way it should feel.

Knob puts a vintage amplifier knob on your desktop — brass, ridged edges, and a glowing LED ring that sweeps from green to red. Scroll to adjust your Mac's volume, click to mute, and drag it anywhere on any monitor.

It lives in the menu bar, stays under a percent of CPU, and even clicks softly as you turn it. A small instrument, patiently tuned.

Visit knob.nikhco.in →

New · For Mac

Mantra

One line, always on your desktop.

Mantra is a tiny widget for one short note — an affirmation, a mantra, a reminder of what really matters. Write it once and it stays put, on the desktop or in Notification Center, in six themes, three text sizes and two alignments.

Free. Works offline. Collects nothing. A Mac App Store release is in progress.

Visit mantra.nikhco.in →

In development · For iPhone

TripWallet

Every border, one wallet.

TripWallet replaces the folder of documents a frequent traveler carries. Passports, visas, insurance and arrival cards — scanned, encrypted on your iPhone, and always viewable offline. Trips build themselves from a flight number, complete with entry requirements linked to official sources.

And for immigrants, nomads and snowbirds: an immigration day counter that replaces the spreadsheet — Schengen 90/180, PR obligations and citizenship countdowns, computed correctly. No accounts. No ads. Ever.

Coming soon — tripwallet.nikhco.in →

For Mac

Fin.

A quiet to-do list, right in your menu bar.

Fin. is a free, minimalist task manager that lives in your Mac menu bar. No accounts. No AI. No subscriptions. Just a clean, dependable way to keep track of what needs doing — with color-coded lists, subtasks, keyboard shortcuts, and iCloud sync across your Macs.

Built natively in Swift. Two megabytes on disk. An homage to the beloved Anxiety app from 2007, rebuilt for modern macOS.

Visit fin.nikhco.in →

On the web

TempDeals

The day's best deals, without the noise.

TempDeals gathers the most interesting bargains from the web's leading coupon and deal sites, then presents them in a calm, filterable feed. No pop-ups. No dark patterns. No five redirects to reach a link — just a thoughtful place to find something worth buying.

Visit tempdeals.net →

Browser tools

TempMarks

Temporary bookmarks, done properly.

Some links only matter for a few hours — a recipe, a receipt, a tab you'll act on tomorrow. TempMarks gives you a separate drawer for those links, so your real bookmarks stay clean and meaningful.

TempNote

A scratchpad, right where you browse.

TempNote is a lightweight notepad that sits in the corner of your browser. Good for addresses, order numbers, and the little bits of text you need for the next five minutes but not for the next five years.
